While you’re there

On the ground

Before you fly
Thursday Nov 26, at SEA check-in, ask whether the checked bags tag through to Delhi. That question is before the trip, not a Monday step.
Navigation
Use Naver Maps. Google Maps is unreliable in Korea. Papago for translation, k.ride for taxis.
Into the city
Friday: limousine bus 6001 or 6015 from Incheon T1 Gate 5 / stop 5B to Chungmuro. ₩17,000 each, about 60–90 minutes. Card is fine.
Out to the airport
Monday is decided. Leave the apartment about 8:55 AM for Seoul Station B2. Buy the AREX Express ticket, then Korean Air check-in, bag drop, and departure immigration. Check-in cuts off at 9:25 AM. The Express is about 10:00–10:30 AM to Terminal 2, about 51 minutes, ₩13,000 each, a separate ticket. At Incheon the designated lane bypasses check-in and immigration; security screening still happens after it.
Climate Card
Climate Card, not T-money, so there is no cash top-up. ₩13,000 a person: ₩3,000 for the card plus ₩10,000 for 3 days. Buy with a foreign card at a Line 1–8 station machine. First tap Saturday. Covers subway and city buses. The AREX Express and the airport limousine are not covered.
Cash
Convert $100 on Friday evening in Myeongdong. The changers are on Myeongdong 2-gil by the Chinese Embassy, and they need a passport. Carry ₩120,000 for two, in small bills, for food stalls only: Friday Myeongdong, then Saturday Gwangjang, then Sunday Hongdae. The leftover, about ₩14,000, pays one AREX Express ticket in cash on Monday. Buy the second ticket by card.
Tax refunds
Passport in hand for instant refunds at Olive Young, Lotte, and Blue Elephant. At Incheon T2, choose the card refund so it lands in USD. Keep tax-free goods unopened.

Practical information

On the ground

Before departure
At check-in at Seattle (SEA) on Thursday, November 26, confirm whether checked baggage will be tagged through to Delhi.
Navigation
Use Naver Maps for navigation; Google Maps has limited functionality in Korea. Papago is recommended for translation and k.ride for taxis.
Into the city
Friday: limousine bus 6001 or 6015 from Incheon Terminal 1, Gate 5 / stop 5B, to Chungmuro. The fare is ₩17,000 per person and the journey takes approximately 60–90 minutes. Card payment is accepted.
Out to the airport
Monday: depart the apartment at approximately 8:55 AM for Seoul Station, Level B2. Purchase AREX Express tickets, then complete Korean Air check-in, baggage drop, and departure immigration. Check-in closes at 9:25 AM. The Express departs at approximately 10:00–10:30 AM for Terminal 2; the journey takes approximately 51 minutes and costs ₩13,000 per person on a separate ticket. At Incheon, the designated lane bypasses check-in and immigration; security screening follows.
Climate Card
The Climate Card is used instead of T-money and does not require cash top-ups. The cost is ₩13,000 per person: ₩3,000 for the card and ₩10,000 for three days. It can be purchased with a foreign card at Line 1–8 station machines, with first use on Saturday. The card covers the subway and city buses; the AREX Express and airport limousine bus are not covered.
Cash
Cash is required for market and street-food stalls. Currency exchanges on Myeongdong 2-gil, near the Chinese Embassy, require a passport. Approximately ₩120,000 in small denominations covers the stalls in Myeongdong (Friday), Gwangjang (Saturday), and Hongdae (Sunday).
Tax refunds
Present a passport for instant refunds at Olive Young, Lotte, and Blue Elephant. At Incheon Terminal 2, select a card refund for payment in USD. Keep tax-free purchases unopened.
